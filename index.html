Current note

A living archive for virtual art practice.

Ars Virtua began as a new media center and gallery in synthetic worlds, supporting artists, audiences, exhibitions, residencies, and research across networked spaces.

The surviving site now acts as both a project directory and a record of early work in Second Life, World of Warcraft, Minecraft, and related virtual environments.
