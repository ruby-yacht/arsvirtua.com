Not a Hero

Not a Hero is an experiment in roleplaying, carried out by John Bruneau. Dave is the character John became.

For Dave, the fantasy is banal. He lives in a world of paperwork and office politics, not demon slaying and magic. His search for ordinary needs inside a fantasy world frames the project.
